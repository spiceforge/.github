<summary><h2 style="display: inline-block; color: #C5D86D;"> SpiceForge Startup & Team Principles</h2></summary>

<br/>

> *Our operating system for building legendary products.*

<table>

1. **Fall in love with the problem, not the solution**  
   *Product-market fit comes through iteration, not planning*

2. **Fire fast, hire slow**

3. **If you're not failing, you're not trying hard enough**

4. **Make decisions, even with imperfect information**

5. **Disrupt inefficient markets**

6. **Focus, focus, focus** — one user, one problem at a time

7. **The CEO is the Chief Storyteller**

8. **Master the art of raising capital** — timing, investor-fit, dilution

9. **Build a great team** — culture beats skill

10. **Understand your users directly**, not through proxies

11. **Go global from day one**, especially from a small home market

12. **Know when to sell — and when not to**

13. **Marketing and brand are community-driven**

14. **Build for the user, not for the press**

15. **Crisis management** — always be ready for survival mode

16. **In meetings pushback is key**  
    *We challenge each other professionally*

17. **Internally, slides exist to explain**  
    *Optics will never matter internally*

</table>
